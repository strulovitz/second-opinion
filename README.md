# Second Opinion

**A medical knowledge globe built only from textbooks.**

Second Opinion is an open-source project that builds a navigable map of
medicine out of 42 standard medical textbooks. Nothing else is used: no
Wikipedia, no drug database, no FDA or WHO list, no general AI knowledge.
If a fact is not on a page of one of the books, it is not in Second Opinion.

Live site (under construction): <https://www.strulovitz.org/2nd-opinion/>

## The idea

The map has two axes, and they are answers to two different questions:

- **Where does it come from?** The geography is the *library itself*. Each
  textbook is a continent, its parts are provinces, its chapters are
  districts. A disease does not belong to one chapter — it *lights up* in
  every territory where the books discuss it.
- **Which is it?** The altitude is the *kind* of thing. Diseases form the
  planet body, symptoms the atmosphere above it, treatments the orbits
  further out, ordered from lifestyle changes through to surgery. Every drug
  gets its own shell.

Between them: every single statement carries its **provenance** — the book,
the chapter, the printed page, and the verbatim sentence it came from. A fact
without a citation is deleted, not displayed.

## Architecture

- **Neo4j** is the single source of truth (Law 4). One graph database holds
  every book, territory, entity, lit spot and edge.
- **kitchen/** holds the Python scripts that build the database, run the
  local free AI "reader" over the page images, and export the result. It runs
  only on the maintainer's laptop. Book PDFs, page images and transcripts are
  never committed.
- **site/** is a fully static website exported *from* the database. No build
  step, no server, no database needed to view it.

## Not medical advice

Second Opinion is not a doctor and is not medical advice. Its content was
generated by AI models reading medical textbooks and may contain mistakes. It
is offered for information only, as another angle to think about. Always
verify with the cited book pages and with a qualified professional you trust.
