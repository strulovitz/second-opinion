Second Opinion

A medical knowledge globe built only from textbooks.

Under construction. The map is being built now, out of 37 medical textbooks and nothing else. No Wikipedia, no drug database, no general AI knowledge. If a fact is not on a page of one of the books, it is not here — and every statement that does appear will carry the book, the chapter, the printed page and the exact sentence it came from.

Explore the 3-D globe

What it will be

  • Where does it come from? The geography is the library itself: each textbook is a continent, its parts are provinces, its chapters are districts.
  • Which is it? The altitude is the kind of thing: diseases at the centre, symptoms above them, treatments further out, from lifestyle changes through to surgery.
  • Side effects are first-class citizens — never hidden, never summarised away.

Follow along

The whole project is open source, including the dated ledger of what is done and what is not: github.com/strulovitz/second-opinion. The map you see will be a static export of a graph database that runs only on the maintainer's laptop.

Second Opinion is not a doctor and is not medical advice. Its content was generated by AI models reading medical textbooks and may contain mistakes. It is offered for information only, as another angle to think about. Always verify with the cited book pages and with a qualified professional you trust.
