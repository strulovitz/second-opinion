## Verbatim quotations

Short verbatim excerpts from the 42 medical textbooks appear throughout the
project as **proving evidence**: each one is stored with its book, its
printed page number and the surrounding sentence, exactly as the books state
it. These excerpts are quoted for identification and verification only. They
remain the copyright of their respective publishers and authors, and are not
placed under CC BY 4.0. No textbook is redistributed by this repository: the
PDFs live only on the owner's computer and are never committed.

## What is AI-generated

The simplified explanations (`tldr` and `eli5` fields) are written by AI
models reading the textbooks and are labelled as AI-written wherever they
appear, per the project's first law: every statement is either verbatim from
a cited page, or visibly marked as generated.
